About Us

U. M. Jintanwala & Co. is a well-established, peer-reviewed Chartered Accountancy firm headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Founded in 1985, the firm brings more than four decades of professional experience in accounting, assurance, taxation, corporate law, finance and regulatory compliance. We provide practical and personalised professional services to businesses, financial institutions, start-ups and other organisations.

Our services cover income tax and GST advisory and compliance, company-law matters, statutory and internal audits, bank audits, risk-based internal audits, project finance, forensic audits and investigations, information systems audits, and representation before tax authorities and other regulatory bodies.

With a strong understanding of Gujarat’s business environment and an emphasis on professional integrity, quality and timely delivery, we assist clients in meeting their accounting, taxation, audit, finance and compliance requirements. Our approach combines established professional practices with the effective use of technology to provide responsive and reliable solutions suited to each client’s requirements.

For over four decades, U. M. Jintanwala & Co. has remained committed to building long-term professional relationships and supporting clients through evolving business, financial and regulatory requirements.
