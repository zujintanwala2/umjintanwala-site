About Us

U. M. Jintanwala & Co. is a well-established, peer-reviewed Chartered Accountancy firm headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Founded in 1985, we bring more than four decades of experience in accounting, assurance, taxation, corporate law, finance and regulatory advisory. We provide practical, end-to-end professional services to businesses, financial institutions, start-ups and other organisations across India.

Led by experienced Chartered Accountants with credentials including FCA, DISA, CISA, FAFD, CCCAB and ISO lead-auditor qualifications, our services cover income tax and GST advisory and compliance, company-law matters, statutory and internal audits, bank audits, risk-based internal audits, project finance, forensic audits and investigations, systems audits, and representation before tax authorities and regulatory bodies.

Our dedicated Governance, Privacy & Controls (GPC) division supports organisations in strengthening governance, protecting data and managing technology and regulatory risk. We provide DPDPA readiness assessments, data mapping, DPIAs, privacy-policy and data-principal-rights governance, breach-response readiness, and privacy audit support.

Our GPC capabilities also include ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27701 and ISO/IEC 42001 readiness and implementation support; information-systems, cloud and IT General Controls reviews; cyber-governance and technology-risk oversight; AI-governance assessments; vendor and third-party risk reviews; policy and control-framework development; and regulatory compliance advisory for entities governed by RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and IFSCA. We assist organisations through gap assessments, control and evidence mapping, risk assessments, remediation roadmaps and audit-readiness reviews.

Combining deep knowledge of Gujarat’s business environment with national and international standards, we deliver personalised, technology-enabled solutions that support sustainable growth, stronger controls and regulatory confidence. Whether the requirement involves routine compliance, complex assurance, privacy readiness or emerging technology risk, U. M. Jintanwala & Co. is a trusted partner for accounting, audit, governance, privacy and compliance services in Ahmedabad and across India.
